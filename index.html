Mission overview

Scope of work — The AFD Green PFM Phase II Feasibility Study mission (April–May 2026) aims to design a modular policy reform matrix of Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) to anchor a future AFD budget support operation to Rwanda.

My scope of work, conducted alongside a team of consultants, revolved around two axes and two principal beneficiaries:
  • Axis A — Green Central Banking, with the National Bank of Rwanda (BNR) — covering monetary policy climate integration and net-zero central banking.
  • Axis B — Green Financial Products & Markets, with MINECOFIN — covering sustainable debt instruments, climate insurance, and green capital markets.

Policy Reform Matrix — (Potential) Disbursement-Linked Indicators

As agreed in the inception report (Section 5.3), the DLI matrix is presented as a "menu of options" to let AFD and the Government of Rwanda calibrate ambition during final negotiations.

The following 13 DLIs focus on the two axes. DLIs outlined in red dotted border are the mission-preferred options recommended for inclusion in the Phase II programme.

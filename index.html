Mission overview

This report presents the findings of the Green PFM Phase II feasibility mission conducted in Kigali, Rwanda in April–May 2026 by Yoan Raih (consultant, Expertise France) on behalf of AFD. The mission builds on the results of Phase I and assesses the feasibility of a second budget support programme aimed at greening Rwanda's financial system.

The scope of the programme crystallised around two axes and two principal beneficiaries:

Axis A — Green Central Banking
National Bank of Rwanda (BNR) — monetary policy climate integration, net-zero central banking, reserves management.
Axis B — Green Financial Products & Markets
MINECOFIN (with CMA, RSE as line institutions) — sustainable debt, climate insurance, capital markets.

Phase II budget support envelope: ~€40M · Technical assistance (Expertise France): ~€1.5M
